      How the levels are estimated
      • Data comes only from Bybit's public API: 15-minute candles and open-interest history for each coin, with prices streamed live.
      • Each 15-minute candle where open interest rose counts as new positions opened at that candle's average price. A green candle leans the new positions long, a red one leans them short.
      • New positions are spread across the leverage mix you pick, capped at the coin's max leverage, and get Bybit's isolated liquidation price: longs at entry × (1 − 1/leverage + maintenance margin), shorts at entry × (1 + 1/leverage − maintenance margin).
      • When open interest falls, every open position shrinks by the same share. Any level that price has already traded through is removed.
      • What's left is grouped in 0.1% steps and smoothed. The cluster shown is the closest peak within 15% that reaches your threshold (30% of the strongest peak on that side by default).
      • These are estimates from one exchange, so read each level as a zone of about ±0.5%. Dollar sizes cover Bybit only. New positions are added as each 15-minute candle closes; prices, distances and sweeps update live.
